## Accounting definitions in the repaired measurement run

- **scope:** IDs frozen into the run snapshot.
- **retrieved:** snapshot IDs whose current persisted row was found.
- **eligible/due:** retrieved IDs whose issued contract is due for this evaluation.
- **evaluated:** item rows successfully processed this run.
- **pending:** not yet due or remaining after the cursor checkpoint.
- **ambiguous:** sufficient coverage but ordering cannot be resolved under the declared contract.
- **unverified:** insufficient inputs/coverage to grade.
- **excluded:** not eligible under the frozen contract.
- **failed:** per-item exception/provider failure; IDs remain listed and retry pending.

A run is COMPLETE only when the stable cursor reaches the frozen cohort and failed IDs are empty. PARTIAL is always visible.
