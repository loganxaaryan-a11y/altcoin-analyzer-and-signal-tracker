## Automatic top-500 discovery (2026-10-01 edit)
- A scheduled `runMarketDiscovery` action reads five ranked CoinGecko market pages (up to 500 assets), current CoinGecko trending attention, and the complete Hyperliquid perpetual context list. It ranks candidates by measured multi-horizon displacement, 24-hour turnover relative to market cap, current attention, open-interest scale, and funding extremes. The resulting 30-row shortlist is stored in `discovery_signals`; alignment is explicitly an evidence-density score, not a win probability.
- The same run executes three public web searches covering broad market developments, crypto sectors, and institutional/market-structure activity. Exact result URLs are retained from the runtime search response. Inference annotates only those supplied titles/snippets with why-it-matters, directional sector impacts, supported asset tags, and direct theories that cite both measured signal indices and linked source indices.
- News impact rows are stored in `discovery_news`. Direct theories that pass measured-signal, math, narrative, and linked-news layers are stored in the existing machine Theory Watch, with countercases, observable invalidations, and verified-vs-inference ripple hops.
- The latest completed scan is read from the artifact database on open; opening the artifact does not initiate market/news research. A six-hour artifact-owned schedule refreshes it, and Run now remains available for an explicit manual refresh. Stored discovery rows are retained for 14 days.
- Sector labels on signal rows are market-cap buckets (`Top 25`, `Top 100`, `Top 500`), not inferred industry classifications. Industry-sector impacts appear only when linked news evidence and bounded inference support them.

## Rating calibration and audit trail (2026-10-01 edit)
- Every dossier alignment rating now carries its complete factor ledger: observed reading, source, signed factor reading, explicit weight, and weighted contribution. The displayed formula is `50 + (weighted net / total weight) × 42`, clamped to 5–95; the opposite-side score is its complement.
- Dossier rating reliability is calculated only from closed tracked signal-horizon observations in the same fixed band (0–49, 50–64, 65–79, 80–100). A target close is a hit and an invalidation close is a miss. Open/pending outcomes do not enter hit rate. Bands with zero closed observations display `UNCALIBRATED`; bands below 30 observations display `LIMITED` rather than implying stable calibration.
- Discovery ratings use a seven-dimension 100-point formula (2026-10-04 edit): Trend 20, Momentum 15, Volatility 10, size-aware spot-volume participation 15, funding crowding 10, network fundamentals (TVL trend) 15, supply overhang 15; pass/full, mixed/half, fail or unavailable/zero. Direction is picked first by a weighted multi-horizon vote (1H×0.5, 24H×1, 7D×1.5, 14D×1.5, 30D×2.5, 200D×4; net ≥3 long, ≤−3 short, else watch); both sides are then scored and the stronger side's read is shown, with watch capped at low/monitor. Trend keeps its 7D+30D displacement bonus and now also credits an established uptrend (30D positive with 14D/200D support) even when 7D is flat. Momentum reads 24H+7D — 1H noise is excluded by design and can no longer zero the dimension. Volume bars are tiered by market cap (≥$10B: 3%/1.5%; $1–10B: 6%/3%; $100M–1B: 10%/5%; <$100M: 12%/4%) with a one-level upgrade for deep perp OI (≥$25M). Neutral funding scores "neutral": zero contribution, labeled no-signal, never a penalty and never a tier blocker. The two fundamentals dimensions are direction-agnostic context; they never enter the severe-gate tier count. Each scheduled scan now closes 5D, 14D, and 30D directional observations at the first scan on or after maturity, using the latest CoinGecko price for the same asset. Directionally positive return is a hit; zero/negative is a miss. These outcomes calibrate only discovery ratings and are never mixed with dossier ratings.
- Discovery signals are retained for 120 days so 30-day cohorts can mature. Reliability text includes band, hit/miss count, sample size, and horizon basis next to the rating throughout the scanner, home feed, tracker, dossier, and watchlist. Untracked qualitative tiers and timing verdicts are explicitly labeled outcome-uncalibrated rather than presented as proven reliability.

## Weekly fundamentals scoring (2026-10-04 edit)
- `refreshFundamentalsScores` scores Tier 1 weekly (direction-agnostic 0–100): TVL trend 7d/30d (DeFiLlama, 30), revenue yield = annualized fees / market cap (DeFiLlama fees, 20), supply overhang = FDV/mcap premium (CoinGecko, 25), developer velocity = GitHub 12-week commit activity via the dossier-populated cache (15), liquidity = size-tiered spot turnover (10). Missing data is NO_DATA and excluded from the blend; coverage (% of factor weight measured) is shown per row. Scores are size-aware (tiered bars) and category-labeled (protocol vs chain TVL scope); L1 TVL, perp-DEX revenue and memecoin activity are not forced onto one axis. Unlock-calendar timing has no verified free feed: FDV/mcap issuance overhang stands in for unlock pressure, and buyback-vs-issuance is reported NO_DATA rather than estimated.
- `getFundamentalsRanking` serves the "Strongest fundamentals" view: weekly scores are kept, while supply-overhang and liquidity sub-scores are recomputed from fresh market data on every read (the daily refresh). Both timestamps — weekly scored-at and market-data refreshed-at — are shown; calling the score daily would be theater. Assets rank only with ≥50% of factor weight measured; thinner-coverage assets are listed separately as unranked so a thin blend can never outrank a fully-measured one. The view feeds the coiled watchlist: strong fundamentals with no quant flags yet are watched, not ignored.
- The dossier factor ledger also scores four fundamentals factors (TVL trend, revenue linkage, developer velocity, supply overhang as a negative-only factor) with the same NO_DATA discipline.

## Discipline and cycle-method upgrades (2026-10-02 edit)
- Signal and journal scorecards now compute expectancy from closed outcomes as `(hit rate × average winning R) − (miss rate × average losing R)`; win rate is secondary. Negative-expectancy buckets are visibly flagged for review and size-down/kill discipline, never auto-traded.
- The former on-demand sizing ladder was retired with the terminal deep-analysis action on 2026-10-06. The live terminal still keeps its existing signal and risk rules unchanged.
- New dossier signals require a 20-observation walk-forward test over cached daily history before a confidence label is earned or a row is persisted. Weekly decision points use the 50D/200D trend direction, evaluate 30 days forward in R units, and report rising-200D versus falling-200D hit rates and expectancy. Narrative/statistics divergence is flagged when the qualified historical expectancy is non-positive.
- `checkentry` applies a hard 5:1 reward/risk gate from the planned entry to the stored invalidation and target. Journal analytics also exposes an editable daily R loss-limit alert in the UI.
- Hyperliquid wallet labels are descriptive heuristics only, derived from the latest 50 already-returned public fills and current positions (closed-fill hit rate, observed hold time, and 48-hour buy/sell notional). They never claim owner identity or event foresight.
- The former terminal research synthesis was retired on 2026-10-06; external research retains the same source-priority doctrine and cannot override measured terminal data.
- BTC halving cycles are aligned by calendar day since each real halving and normalized to the first cached close. Similarity uses weekly matched normalized-return observations, Pearson correlation, and return-path RMSE. A cross-cycle pattern requires at least two complete prior cycles, at least 26 weekly matches each, and correlation ≥0.60 in both; otherwise the UI explicitly states that no statistically meaningful pattern exists. Divergence requires two analogs before it can flag.

- `runCrossSynthesis` composes a single evidence ledger from current 91-day BTC/ETH/SOL/LINK CoinGecko history, current CoinGecko category samples, the latest stored OKX funding/open-interest snapshots, live screened Hyperliquid leaderboard positions, and the latest stored five-gate radar and linked discovery-news rows.

Missing lanes are kept in the source-health record and cause a degraded input-set label rather than an inferred substitute.

## Theory integrity gates (2026-10-02 edit)
- A machine theory’s key factual premise is multi-source only when at least two distinct publisher names are attached. Fewer than two publishers produces a durable `SINGLE SOURCE` flag in the stored theory snapshot, caps confidence at low, marks the news layer failed, and disables automatic invalidation.
- Directional price tripwires are computed only from the theory’s own linked asset and direction. A bullish tripwire uses the lowest of the prior 20 daily closes and must be strictly below the current price; a bearish tripwire uses the highest prior close and must be strictly above the current price. The snapshot stores the level, current price, side, source, window, verification timestamp, and `UNBREACHED` state.
- If the required history or correct-side level is unavailable, the theory keeps its written falsification conditions and explicitly stores no price tripwire. The engine never substitutes another signal’s invalidation level.
- Automatic invalidation now requires both multi-source eligibility and a stored, creation-verified tripwire that the current close has actually breached. Trend-vote changes alone no longer invalidate a theory.
- The integrity audit checks existing theories for single-source foundations, wrong-side or already-breached-at-creation levels, and invalidated rows lacking valid tripwire evidence. Unsupported invalidations are reverted to live/contested, tripwires are recomputed from current linked-asset history when possible, and every repair is recorded in the theory snapshot.

## DeFiLlama level-3 derivations (2026-10-03 edit)
- **TVL momentum and inflections:** protocol and chain histories are normalized to positive dated observations. The layer reports recomputable 7D and 30D change rates and acceleration (`7D − 30D`). The latest one-day TVL percentage delta is standardized against up to 90 preceding daily deltas; at least 30 trailing samples are required. An inflection is flagged only when the 7D and 30D rates have opposite signs and the latest delta z-score is beyond ±1.5σ. Missing history or a non-breaching move is explanatory context, not a failure.
- **TVL / market-cap context:** for a mapped protocol, current TVL is divided by the current `mcap` in `https://api.llama.fi/protocols`. Its percentile is ranked against up to 90 dated protocol-TVL observations using the same current market-cap denominator. The constant-denominator approximation is disclosed in the returned methodology; at least 30 observations and a positive market cap are required. This is valuation context only and is never promoted to a buy/sell signal.
- **DEX flow versus price:** when the mapped chain has a usable row at `https://api.llama.fi/overview/dexs/{chain}?excludeTotalDataChart=true&excludeTotalDataChartBreakdown=true`, the 7D DEX-volume change is read directly or recomputed from `total7d` and `total14dto7d`. It is compared with the asset's 7D CoinGecko daily-price change. Opposite non-zero directions are labeled divergence; aligned directions are labeled aligned; a missing chain row, missing prior period, or missing price remains `NO_DATA`.
- All three derivations appear inline in the existing on-chain panel and in `DEFI ACTIVITY · LEVEL 3` theory evidence with source and as-of timestamps. They carry `factorEligible: false` and do not enter factor scores. Existing DeFi TVL direction can still be checked in its evidence layer, but missing data never becomes `FAIL`.
- Supply-overhang modeling remains deliberately open: DeFiLlama `/emissions` is paywalled and no verified free unlock source is substituted. The Binance Futures path is unchanged; its regional HTTP 451 remains honest `NO_DATA`.

## Cross-venue derivatives aggregation (2026-10-04 edit)
- **Deribit public API v2:** `https://www.deribit.com/api/v2/public/` supplies BTC/ETH perpetual ticker funding, USD open interest and mark price plus option book summaries. Front month is the nearest unexpired listed expiry. The 25-delta call and put are selected by closest Black-Scholes delta from each summary's underlying price and mark IV; risk reversal is call IV minus put IV. Put/call volume is the front-expiry 24-hour contract-volume ratio, and front IV is the mean mark IV of the nearest-strike call and put. Missing or unsupported instruments return `NO_DATA` and never block the dossier.
- **Kraken Futures public API v3:** `https://futures.kraken.com/derivatives/api/v3/` supplies the `/tickers` feed for `PI_` perpetual contracts. BTC maps only to `PI_XBTUSD`, ETH to `PI_ETHUSD`, and other symbols require an exact returned `PI_{SYMBOL}USD` row. Kraken funding is one-hour and is multiplied by eight for the comparable 8-hour blend; both native period and normalized value remain visible.
- **OKX public API:** `https://www.okx.com/api/v5/` remains the primary history-bearing source for current funding, current OI, 24-hour OI change and taker flow.
- Funding is normalized to eight hours and weighted by each available venue's current USD open interest. The funding spread is max minus min across available venues; values above 0.01% are flagged as `venue-specific crowding`. Unsupported or unreachable venues are excluded, not zero-filled.
- Total OI sums current available venue values. Aggregate 24-hour OI change is weighted only across venues with a verified baseline, and the UI shows what percentage of current aggregate OI that baseline covers. The OI-vs-price regime is mechanical: rising price + rising OI = `new money in`; rising price + flat/falling OI = `short covering`; falling price + rising OI = `longs building into weakness`; falling price + flat/falling OI = `deleveraging`.
- The 30-day crowding comparison uses up to 90 recent normalized funding observations returned by OKX and Hyperliquid, weighted by current OI. It requires at least 20 observations. It can add a timing caution in `checkentry`, but never changes the stored 0–100 dossier score.
