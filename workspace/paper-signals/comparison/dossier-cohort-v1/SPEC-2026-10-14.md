# Dossier Prospective Cohort — v1 Spec

**Cohort id:** `dossier-cohort-v1`
**Cohort version:** 1
**Written:** 2026-10-14 ~14:37 ET (2026-10-14T18:37Z)
**Status:** prospective, collecting

## Why this cohort exists

The expert ordered a measurement pivot (2026-10-14): the **dossier engine** is what Logan
actually sees and trades off, so its recommendations need their **own prospective
cohort**, separately versioned from the frozen paper trial
(production-vs-C-2, freeze v2.1.5). The frozen paper trial continues untouched —
nothing in this cohort alters, renames, or reinterprets any frozen record.

## Scope — what enters the cohort

- **Population:** every new **dossier recommendation** (factor-agreement dossier verdict
  published by the crypto terminal, i.e. rows the terminal writes to `generated_signals`
  through the dossier path) with **decision time at or after 2026-10-14 14:35 ET
  (18:35 UTC)**.
- **INCLUDING** any recommendation sent to chat (Logan's alerts, side-chat posts,
  notifications): chat-sent recommendations are dossier recommendations Logan could
  act on and must not be missed.
- **NO backfill.** Dossier signals decided before the cutoff (including the 2026-10-14
  09:00 ET longs, recorded here only as seed entries with honest expired-non-entry
  status) do not count as live cohort calls.
- **NO historical rewrites.** The cohort never edits or re-scores a historical record.

## Per-record payload (what each row carries)

Every row carries **decision-time evidence**, exactly as it stood when the dossier
committed:

- Identity: `dossier_row_id`, `terminal` (crypto), `symbol`, `coin_id`,
  `dossier_signal_id` (the terminal's `generated_signals` id), `cohort_id`,
  `cohort_version`.
- Timing: `decision_at_utc` (the decision instant; `decision_time_verified` marks
  whether decisionAt was persisted by the terminal or derived), `issued_at_utc`,
  `source_as_of_utc`, `window_start_utc` / `window_end_utc`.
- The call: `direction`, `confidence`, `setup_type`, `entry`, `invalidation`,
  `target1`, `target2`, `atr14`, `risk_multiplier`, `risk_method`.
- The evidence: the full 11-factor dossier ledger as committed at decision time —
  per factor: label, score, contribution, quality (`live` / `no_data`), `asOf`,
  source, and reading/value. Stored verbatim in `factor_ledger_json`; anything the
  terminal did not persist is marked `UNAVAILABLE`, never invented.
- `notes` for provenance caveats (e.g. missing factor asOf, unlabeled inputs).

## Entry contract (executable entry conditions)

Each row records the executable conditions Logan could have used:

- **Entry rule:** the dossier's stated entry level (the `entry` field), evaluated under
  the frozen `actionable-entry-v1` entry contract semantics —
  `entryWindowOpensAt = decisionAt`, **`entryWindowExpiresAt = decisionAt + 15 min`**
  (crypto), `void_expired` on any print after window end, drift cap 1.50 crypto,
  5-min observation-age limit. (Frozen reference:
  `freeze-manifest-v2.1.0.json` `operational_rules.entry_contract`; receipt-expiry
  parity amendment v2.1.5: `received_at > window_end -> ('fail',
  'abstain:entry-expired')`.)
- **Invalidation:** the dossier's `invalidation` level.
- **Targets:** `target1` / `target2` as published.
- **Horizon:** the dossier publishes no explicit holding horizon; settlement uses
  the outcome rule below.

## Entry disposition at record time (honest labeling)

A dossier recommendation is **not** an automatic cohort entry. At record time each
row is labeled:

- `entered` — an entry print was observed inside the window under the entry contract
  (evidence: entry print venue + timestamps), or
- `expired_non_entry` — the 15-min window closed with no entry observed
  (`void_expired` semantics), evidence retained, OR
- `void_drift` / `void_no_quote` — the frozen terminal states, with reasons.

Expired/voided rows stay in the file as non-entries. They are excluded from the
accuracy cohort but never deleted — a dead window is data.

## Outcome-settlement rule (documented choice)

**Chosen: the same first-touch 5D/14D/30D convention as the frozen paper trial,
net of the frozen 18 bps cost model.**

Rationale: the expert's pivot is comparative — the dossier engine must be measured
against the paper trial on identical rails. A dossier-specific horizon
(e.g. "until target1 touch or invalidation") would make the two cohorts
incomparable, defeating the purpose of the pivot. The dossier's invalidation
level remains the standing per-call invalidation for first-touch grading, exactly
as the paper trial grades it.

Consequences:
- `outcome_5 / outcome_14 / outcome_30`, `return_5 / return_14 / return_30` graded
  first-touch (invalidation vs target1), net of 18 bps round-trip, per the frozen
  evaluator semantics.
- Outcome columns are `not-graded` until their checkpoint elapses. Never scored as
  0, never silently dropped.
- **Paper signals only. No brokerage. No model tuning** — this cohort measures; it
  does not train.

## Cost treatment

- Frozen 18 bps round-trip model, applied net to every graded outcome — identical
  to the frozen paper trial, so cost treatment is never a hidden difference
  between the cohorts.

## Cross-population rule (dossier vs paper trial)

- A dossier recommendation that **also** appears as a paper-trial signal is recorded
  in **both** cohorts. **Dossier signals never silently enter the paper cohort.**
- Populations link only: `cross_population_link` on the dossier row carries the
  paper-trial row's identifier (e.g. `candidate_id` / `contract_id`), and vice
  versa if the paper collector supports it. Linking is a join key, not a merge.
- Neither cohort's counts, verdicts, or dispositions may be read across the link.
  The dossier cohort measures the dossier engine; the paper cohort measures the
  paper trial. Independence is preserved.

## Versioning

- This spec is **cohort v1**. Any change to inclusion rules, outcome settlement,
  cost treatment, or entry semantics requires a new cohort version (v2, v3, …) with
  a new spec; rows carry `cohort_version` so provenance survives a change.
- Superseded versions are preserved, never edited (same discipline as the frozen
  paper trial's `FREEZE-v*.SUPERSEDED.md` files).
